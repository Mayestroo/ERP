# NOVDA ERP — Coverage & Gap Audit v0.4

**Maqom:** v0.3 arxitekturasi ustidan talablar auditi. Bu hujjat **yakuniy implementatsiya spetsifikatsiyasi emas**.  
**Sana:** 2026-10-11.  
**Audit qilingan hujjat:** `NOVDA_ERP_ENTERPRISE_BLUEPRINT_v0.3.md` (40 ta P01–P40 jarayon, 7 ta FLOW A–G).  
**Vazifa:** Fabrika jarayonlari qamrovidagi aniq bo‘shliqlar, ichki qarama-qarshilik xavfi va qaror qabul qilishdan oldingi tekshiruvlarni to‘plash.

## 0. Audit hukmi

v0.3 **kuchli konseptual karkas**, ammo **to‘liq ERP spetsifikatsiyasi emas**. Unda fizik harakat, QR, ASN, MES/WMS, subcontracting, marketplace va yagona stock ledger tamoyillari bor. Biroq bir nechta bo‘limlar faqat sarlavha yoki umumiy oqim darajasida qolgan. Hech narsa chetda qolmaganini tasdiqlash uchun real sex walkthrough, haqiqiy hujjatlar, foydalaniladigan qurilmalar, ishlab chiqarish modeli va hisobchilik siyosati tekshirilishi zarur.

**Holat belgilari:** `BOR` — v0.3 da ko‘zda tutilgan; `QISMAN` — konsepsiya mavjud, operatsion qoidalar yetishmaydi; `YO‘Q` — mustaqil jarayon/aniq talab sifatida kiritilmagan. `BOR` ishlab bo‘lganini anglatmaydi.

### Muqarrar tamoyillar

1. ERPNext Stock Ledger va General Ledger yagona hisob manbalari; MES/WMS fizik eventlardan **ikkinchi parallel qoldiq** yaratmaydi.
2. Scan, weigh, print va operator kliki alohida evidence; faqat role/validatsiyadan keyingi ERP hujjati moliyaviy yoki stok harakatini amalga oshiradi.
3. Supplier declared kg, NOVDA measured kg, accepted kg, invoiced kg **alohida** saqlanadi.
4. `ROLL` (fizik rulon), `LOT` (partiya), `BND` (tikuvchilik pachkasi), `CTN` (quti) va ERPNext `Serial and Batch Bundle` **turli tushunchalar**. Ular mapping orqali bog‘lanadi.
5. Supplierning o‘z QR kodi, NOVDA ichki QR kodi, GS1 identifikatori, WB/Uzum barcode va qonuniy DataMatrix/KIZ **alohida namespace**; standart yoki kod statusi isbotlanmaguncha ularni almashtirib ishlatish mumkin emas.
6. Tashqi pudratchida turgan material: **owner**, **custodian**, **location**, **valuation**, **quality** va **reservation** alohida belgilanadi.
7. Har bir jarayonda partial / reject / return / retry / reverse / audit variantlari ko‘riladi.

## 2. Tasdiqlanishi shart bo‘lgan end-to-end xaritalar

Bu oqimlar BPMN/swimlane, ekran wireframe, contract, state machine, hujjat harakati va test bilan ko‘rsatiladi:

1. `PLM` — dizayn → namuna → o‘lchov/fit → texkarta/lekalo → BOM/revision → production release.
2. `Demand/MRP` — prognoz/B2B/WB/Uzum → size/color talab → net demand → supply/capacity → buy/make/subcontract.
3. `Procure-to-Pay` — RFQ → PO → advance → supplier roll/ASN → delivery → QC → accepted qty → price/claim → invoice/payment.
4. `Supplier-made fabric` — kimning xomashyosi? → ishlab chiqarish bosqichlari → yield/loss → roll QR → NOVDA acceptance.
5. `Receive-to-Putaway` — gate → roll/HU scan → weigh → quarantine → lab QC → ERP Purchase Receipt/stock → bin.
6. `Cut-to-WIP` — pick roll → lay/cut ticket → consumed kg/metr → recut/defect/remnant → bundle QR → WIP.
7. `Sew-to-FG` — operation assignments → scan → quality → rework → finishing → QC → production receipt.
8. `Outbound Subcontract` — NOVDA WIP → material handover → contractor 1 → contractor 2 (ruxsat bilan) → returns/QC → service invoice.
9. `Inbound Contract Manufacturing` — customer-owned fabric → NOVDA service → finished return → service sale & ownership check.
10. `Pack-to-FBO` — SKU/color/size → unit package/market barcode/KIZ where required → carton → load → carrier → marketplace warehouse accept.
11. `FBS` — buyer order → stock reserve → SKU pick → label → carrier handover → delivery/cancel/return.
12. `B2B/Export` — quotation → contract → allocate → pick/pack → customs if required → customer delivery → invoice/payment.
13. `Reverse/Claim` — WB/UZ/B2B/partner return → physical scan → QC → restock/rework/scrap/credit/recall.
14. `Worker-to-Payroll` — approved operation and rate revision → attendance → piece/day/month wages → adjustments → Hisob import/export → approved payment.
15. `Cost-to-Cash` — material/operation/overhead costs → actual COGS → marketplace invoice/fees/penalties → settlement bank → currency and P&L.
16. `Exception/Recovery` — power outage → offline queue → replay → conflict → manual review → reversal; serial duplicate/invalid mark.
17. `Month-end Close` — inventory count → valuation → accruals → WIP → supplier payable → marketplace receivable → profit & loss.
18. `Equipment/Maintenance` — planned maintenance → parts withdrawal → downtime event → shift/capacity change → repair/QC sign-off.

## 4. Minimal holatlar va cheklovlar

- **ASN:** draft → vendor prepared → sealed/dispatched → arrived → receiving → accepted/partial/disputed → closed.
- **Roll:** announced → supplier labeled → in transit → received-quarantine → QC released/rejected → reserved → cutting issued → part-consumed/remnant/scrap/fully-consumed.
- **Bundle:** cut → assigned → in sewing → waiting QC → rework or accepted → finished. Bundle ichidagi detallar soni fizik birlik sifatida audit qilinadi.
- **Subcontract:** prepared → sent → partner acknowledged → in progress → partial return → NOVDA QC → billed/claim → closed.
- **Finished goods:** pending QC → approved → sellable/reserved → picked → boxed → in transit → marketplace accepted or disputed → sold/returned.
- **Payment status:** customer paid, supplier paid va stock receipt statuslari aralashmaydi.

**Qattiq invariantlar:**

- `DeclaredWeight != MeasuredWeight != AcceptedWeight != InvoicedWeight` bo‘lishi mumkin, farqlar qayd etiladi.
- Bir rulon/lotning sifat holati bekor qilinmaguncha karantindan ishlab chiqarishga chiqishi bloklanadi.
- Bitta fizik birlik ikkita lokatsiyada bir vaqtda bo‘lolmaydi; ikki fizik aktor harakatlari conflict aniqlashi kerak.
- Stock rezervi fizik jo‘natma emas, jo‘natma marketplace sotilgan tovar emas, buyurtma paid settlement emas.
- Bir xil scan/event key bitta ERP postingdan ko‘p yaratmaydi.
- WIP qayta ishlash va scrap uchun alohida quantity event/posting va qayta mehnat qiymati ajratiladi.
- Erkin 'manual quantity override' qoldiqni audit izisiz tuzatmaydi.
- Cost, rate, BOM/techpack yangilanganda oldingi tasdiqlangan hujjatlar silently qayta yozilmaydi.
- Bir marketplace’da sotilgan mahsulot ikkinchi marketplace’da xuddi o‘sha fizik qoldiq sifatida yana sotilmasligi kerak; over-allocation alert va rezerv qoidasi zarur.
- Tizimda shartli **owner** va **custodian** tushunchalari farqlanadi, ayniqsa customer-owned fabric va uslugaga berilgan materiallarda.

## 6. Ulgurji, bozorlar va tannarx formulalari

1. **Real production cost:** actual fabric + accessories + packaging + verified labor + subcontract processing + allocated overhead + attributable scrap/rework + costing policy bo‘yicha other costs.
2. **Marketplace contribution margin:** recognized seller revenue minus platform fees/commission, fulfillment/logistics, channel-funded discounts, attributable advertising, penalties, returned goods loss, COGS, applicable taxes. Amaliy hisoblashni platformalarning moliyaviy hisobotlari va buxgalter tasdiqlagan siyosatga bog‘lash kerak.
3. **Supplier landed cost:** purchase amount plus attributable transport, duty, insurance, and related charges where applicable; ERPNext Landed Cost Voucher mapping tested.
4. **WIP cost:** recognized material consumed, approved operations, subcontract service, overhead; unaccepted WIP and open jobs separate.
5. **FX:** transaction currency, reporting currency, exchange date/source, FX gains/losses, rounding policy recorded; payables, receivables and settlement are distinct.

## 7. Demo emas — haqiqiy UAT uchun test ssenariylari

1. **Partial supplier:** 80 rulondan 78 tasi keldi, biri noto‘g‘ri rang, 2 tasi vazn farqli; PO/ASN/claim va payable mos bo‘lsin.
2. **Weigh:** kalibrovka expire, tarozi unstable, tare error, manual override, reprint — avtomatik kirim bloklanishi yoki ko‘rib chiqish yo‘li bo‘lsin.
3. **Roll split:** 24.00 kg rulondan 9.00 kg kesildi, 14.60 kg remnant va 0.40 kg documented loss — mass balance va lineage tekshirilsin.
4. **Mixed cut:** bitta cutting jobda 5 razmer, ikkita roll/shade; parts count va bundle IDs to‘g‘ri chiqsin.
5. **Piecework:** ikki xodim bitta bundle operatsiyasi bilan ishlaydi; duplicate scan to‘lovni oshirmaydi, rework alohida policy bo‘yicha yuritiladi.
6. **Multi-leg usluga:** 500 bundle 1-partnerga, 400 tasi 2-partnerga (agar ruxsat bo‘lsa), partial return, loss, claim, service invoice bir-biriga yopishsin.
7. **FG packing:** 2-pack aralash rang, noto‘g‘ri size scan, mixed carton va reprint; quti manifesti faktga mos.
8. **WB FBO:** 400 dona jo‘natildi; qabul 393, 7 discrepancy; har 400 ni sotilgan deb ko‘rsatmasin.
9. **FBS:** bekor bo‘lgan buyurtmada rezerv bo‘shatilsin; jo‘natilgan tovarni birinchi omborga qaytarib qo‘ymasin.
10. **Marking:** bir nechta GTIN + bir xil KIZ ikki dona uchun yozilmoqda — hard block va audit.
11. **Return:** 30 dona marketplace qaytimi: 20 sellable, 5 rework, 5 scrap; stok, cost va claim mos keladi.
12. **Migration:** Excel rassenka, Hisobdagi xodimlar, boshlang‘ich qoldiq import; dublikat va identity mapping holatlari qayd etilsin.
13. **Offline:** 8 soat tarmoq yo‘q; barcha sync qilingan yozuvlar bir martadan posted, konfliktlar alohida ko‘rib chiqiladi.
14. **Accounting close:** actual stock, WIP, sold goods COGS, marketplace receivables, vendor payable, payroll accrual va GL reconciliation.
15. **Role isolation:** vendor A vendor B ning roll narxi/partiyasini o‘qiy olmaydi; xodim to‘lovni bir o‘zi o‘zgartira olmaydi.
16. **Recovery:** ma’lumotlar bazasining yangi backupidan alohida muhitga restore qilinadi, stock/GL checksum va sample IDs tekshiriladi.
