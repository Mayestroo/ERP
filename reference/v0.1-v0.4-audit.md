## Cross-version decisions and unresolved discrepancies

1. **Stock identity:** v0.1/v0.2 recommend a batch per roll; v0.4 explicitly distinguishes physical roll, lot, bundle, carton and native Serial and Batch Bundle. Do not freeze the proposed mapping until the actual ERPNext version/native model is inspected and tested. Current status: provisional ADR007.
2. **Receipt/QC sequencing:** v0.1 describes automatic quarantine receipt after validated scale checks; v0.2 makes native Purchase Receipt/QC configuration conditional. Treat quarantine receipt as a proposed target flow only; no stock posting is accepted as proven.
3. **Tolerance and sample values:** all sample quantities, dates, codes and ±0.200 kg example are illustrative. No real-world tolerance, UOM policy, threshold, or label media is inferred.
4. **Scope coverage:** P01–P40 are a useful process inventory but not the required G01–G40 evidence mapping. Seven v0.3 flows and 18 v0.4 headings are not detailed F01–F18 actor flow documents.
5. **Implementation/gates:** all four sources are conceptual documents. There is no verified ERPNext runtime, custom app, native Purchase Receipt/Stock Ledger, physical device, supplier portal, or production workflow evidence in them.
